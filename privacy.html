Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Asli Munafa (asli-munafa.pages.dev)

The short version We don’t collect, store, sell or see any of your data. Your numbers are calculated on your own phone or computer and never leave it.

Who we are

Asli Munafa is a free profit calculator for small businesses, made by Vansh (@itworks_withvansh on Instagram). There is no company behind it and no paid plan.

What we collect

Nothing. The tool has no accounts, no login, no server, no database, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking pixels. The page is built so it can’t send your numbers anywhere: a browser security rule (a Content Security Policy) blocks it from making any network connection after it loads.

Your numbers

  • Everything you type is calculated inside your browser, on your device.
  • By default nothing is saved. Close the page and the numbers are gone.
  • If you turn on Remember on this device, your numbers are saved in your browser’s local storage on that device only. We can’t see them. To delete them, turn the switch off, tap Clear everything, or clear your browser’s site data.
  • Images you save or share are made on your device. They only go somewhere if you choose to send them. The share card shows percentages only, not ₹ amounts.

Hosting

The website’s files are hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Like any website host, Cloudflare handles basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page and protect it from attacks, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. This never includes the numbers you type. We have not turned on any analytics, and we don’t receive visitor logs.

Messages to us

If you message us on Instagram (for example, to ask for monthly tracking), that conversation happens on Instagram under Meta’s privacy policy. We only use it to reply to you, and we never share it.

Offline copy and source code

The downloadable offline file works the same way: it runs only in your browser and can’t connect to the internet. The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check these claims.

Children

This tool is meant for business owners. It doesn’t knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. We will never start collecting your numbers.

Contact

Questions? Send a DM to @itworks_withvansh on Instagram, or open an issue on GitHub.

आपके आंकड़े

  • आप जो भी डालते हैं, उसका हिसाब आपके डिवाइस के ब्राउज़र में ही होता है।
  • शुरू में कुछ सेव नहीं होता। पेज बंद किया तो आंकड़े चले गए।
  • अगर आप इस डिवाइस पर याद रखें चालू करते हैं, तो आंकड़े सिर्फ़ उसी डिवाइस के ब्राउज़र में सेव होते हैं। हम उन्हें नहीं देख सकते। मिटाने के लिए स्विच बंद करें, सब मिटाएं दबाएं, या ब्राउज़र का साइट डेटा साफ़ करें।
  • सेव या शेयर की गई इमेज आपके डिवाइस पर बनती है और तभी कहीं जाती है जब आप भेजें। शेयर कार्ड में सिर्फ़ प्रतिशत होता है, ₹ रकम नहीं।
